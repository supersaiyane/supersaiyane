### What I do

I build the layer between *"production is on fire"* and *"nobody had to wake up."*

- **Reliability at regulated scale** — SLOs, error budgets, and incident response for systems where downtime is a headline.
- **Platform engineering** — golden paths, IDPs, GitOps. Developers ship faster *because* the guardrails exist.
- **AIOps** — LLM-assisted triage and RCA. Machines do first response; humans do judgment.
- **FinOps** — multi-cloud cost intelligence, because unowned spend is a reliability risk wearing a disguise.
